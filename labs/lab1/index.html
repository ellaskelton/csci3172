My Process

Sketch & Visualized

Every project starts with rough sketches and color palettes. I like mapping out the vision before touching any materials.

Plan & Prepare

I like to plan things ahead, such as material cost, fabric size, and overall layout. Taking time to plan upfront helps everything come together more smoothly in the end.

Create

This is where the hands-on work happens — cutting fabric, layering paper mache, and stitching seams. This stage is about turning plans into something physical.

Refine & Finish

The final steps focus on details like painting, pressing seams, and adding small finishing touches that complete the piece.

About Me

Hello, my name is Ella!

I’m a computer science student at Dalhousie University who enjoys combining sewing with coding. I like working with my hands, whether that’s sewing, paper mache, or building apps.

Right now, I'm working on a sewing pattern app centered around a sketch feature that lets you turn 2D designs into simple 3D previews and try out different colors and fabrics. One feature of the app focuses on laying patterns on fabric in a more cost-efficient way, helping reduce waste and use less material. It's still a work in progress, but finishing it this year is one of my goals :)
